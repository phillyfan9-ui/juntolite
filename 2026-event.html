The 2026 Bryan Saylor Memorial Leisure Games

October 3, 2026 · Sly Fox Brewery · Pottstown, PA · Benefiting Julia’s Grace Foundation

32 teams
6 games
Over $15,000 raised in net proceeds

The Event

Thirty-two two-person teams played six single-elimination tournaments: ping pong, beer pong, quoits, cornhole, ladder golf and Kan Jam. Points doubled with each round a team advanced, and the team with the most total points across all six games was crowned overall champion.

Supporting Julia’s Grace Foundation

Junto Lite is hosting the Bryan Saylor Memorial Leisure Games to honor the legacy of Bryan. 100% of net proceeds from the event will support Julia’s Grace Foundation.
