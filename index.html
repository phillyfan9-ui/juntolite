The Spirit of Junto Lite: Growth, Fellowship & Community

In 1727, Benjamin Franklin formed the original “Junto”—a weekly gathering of friends dedicated to mutual improvement, open inquiry, and supporting one another’s personal and professional ambitions. Centuries later, Bryan Saylor brought four friends together to launch Junto Lite, modernizing Franklin’s timeless concept into a modern-day fellowship group designed to share and respectfully debate impactful topics.

Core Pillars

Growth Mindset & Unfiltered Counsel

A confidential sounding board where trusted friends share business strategies, brainstorm technology and future goals, and discuss each individual’s wins and losses. Each gathering is hosted by one member, who provides food and beverages and chooses a topic for the group to discuss and debate.

Curiosity & Lifelong Learning

Every gathering returns to three questions: Where Have You Been? Where Are You Going? What Have You Seen? Together, these make room for reflection, future visions, and ideas from books, podcasts, and life. The conversation helps members turn new perspectives into practical next steps, while remaining open to lessons drawn from both success and struggle.

Community & Fellowship in Action

True to Franklin’s mission to “be serviceable to mankind,” Junto Lite bridges personal growth with community impact through camaraderie, healthy competition, and charitable giving. What began as a small circle of friends has grown into a way to bring people together, celebrate shared purpose, and direct the group’s energy toward causes that matter to the community.

Bryan’s Legacy

Honoring Our Founder, Friend, and Catalyst

Bryan Saylor was the spark that created Junto Lite. He brought us together—Jared, Ben, Jason, and himself—with a clear vision: to create a dedicated space where close friends could challenge each other, brainstorm big ideas, navigate life’s challenges, and celebrate each other’s wins. Bryan lived with curiosity, generosity, and an infectious drive to unite people. He believed that when friends lift each other up, the whole community benefits.

Junto Lite is hosting the Bryan Saylor Memorial Leisure Games to honor the legacy of Bryan. 100% of net proceeds from the event will support Julia’s Grace Foundation, turning our shared memory of Bryan into tangible support for children and families facing childhood cancer.

Bryan Saylor Memorial Leisure Games

Six tournaments to determine one championship team. Every game counts.

Register with one partner and play every game together as a doubles team. Each event is a single elimination tournament where the points awarded for a win double each round.
